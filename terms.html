1. Project scope

Personal Health Insights is an independent, single-user project operated by fropic to review the owner's Oura data. This website provides project information. It does not provide public registration, subscription services, or health-data hosting.

2. Authorized use

The local integration is intended only for data from an Oura account the owner controls and authorizes. Use must respect Oura's API terms, permissions, and access limits. The owner is responsible for maintaining the account, protecting credentials, and choosing the data submitted for analysis.

3. Wellness information

Reports and AI-generated explanations are for personal wellness information. They are not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and the project is not an emergency monitoring service. Measurements and interpretations can be incomplete or inaccurate. Decisions requiring clinical assessment should be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional.

4. Availability and accuracy

Data availability depends on the ring, app synchronization, Oura account permissions, applicable membership, and API availability. No continuous availability or particular analytical outcome is promised. Correlations between habits and measurements do not establish causation.

5. Privacy and third-party services

The privacy notice describes the intended data flow. Oura, GitHub, and any AI service used remain subject to their own terms and policies. Oura and its marks belong to their respective owners; this project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Oura.

6. Stopping use and updates

The owner may stop using the project, revoke Oura access, and delete local records at any time. Provider-side copies are managed separately using the provider's controls. These terms should be updated if the project's functionality or audience changes.

7. Contact

Project maintainer: fropic. Application-related correspondence can use the private contact email provided in the Oura developer registration. Do not share credentials or health records in public channels.
